Everyone Sells the Same AI, Appendix H: Sources and Updates

Last checked: November 6, 2026

This page is the full source list for Appendix H of Everyone Sells the Same AI, "A Worked Eligibility Comparison: Drafting a Credit Memo from Private Side Material." The appendix was verified against providers' public documents as of November 5 to 6, 2026. The printed book lists the key sources; this page keeps the full list. The numbers in brackets below are the same numbers printed in Appendix H, so a reader can look up any bracketed number from the book here. Entries are updated when a cited page changes, and each change is recorded in the change log at the end of this page.

Every verdict in Appendix H is dated and lapses when a cited page changes. Nothing in the comparison is ELIGIBLE on the published documents. It is not a ranking, it names no winner, and it makes no claim about performance, price or quality.

Notes

The author works at AWS. AWS was assessed using the same test as every other provider.

This page, like Appendix H, is provided for informational and educational purposes only and does not constitute legal, investment, financial, or compliance advice.

Before relying on any row, the reader's firm should obtain written confirmation from each provider of the items listed for that row, and have the firm's own counsel read the governing terms.

The views and opinions expressed on this page are those of the author alone and do not necessarily reflect the views, policies, or positions of Amazon Web Services (AWS), Amazon.com, Inc., or any other organization with which the author is affiliated.

How to read the lists

  • Supports names the row and gate of the evidence matrix (Appendix H, Tables H.3 and H.4) that the source supports, with the cell's result as printed: PASS, UNVERIFIED or FAIL. A cell result is not a verdict. "H.7" or "Step 0" means the source is cited in the narrative rather than in a matrix cell.
  • Row labels: 1a Nova 2 Lite; 1b Claude other than Fable 5 and 5.1; 1c Claude Fable 5 and 5.1; 1d OpenAI gpt-oss; 1e OpenAI other than gpt-oss; 1f-1 Llama 3.3 70B Instruct, Kimi K3, Palmyra X5, Grok 4.7; 1f-2 AI21 Labs Jamba 1.5, DeepSeek, Google Gemma, MiniMax, Mistral AI, NVIDIA Nemotron, Qwen, Z.AI GLM 5.3. Rows 1a to 1f are Amazon Bedrock, US Regions only.
  • Accessed is the date the page was read. "November 5 and 6, 2026" means it was read on both days.
